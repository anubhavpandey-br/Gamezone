Tournament Rules & Fair Play

  • All players must join the custom room at least 15 minutes prior to match schedule.

  • Room ID and password will be delivered via SMS/WhatsApp to the registered captain's phone number.
  • Tournament coordinators hold final discretion regarding dispute resolutions.
